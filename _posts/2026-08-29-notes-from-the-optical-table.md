This is a starter blog post. Replace it with a note about a photonic device, an optical measurement, a simulation result, a debugging session, or a paper you are reading.

Good technical posts usually keep three things visible: the question, the evidence, and the decision that followed. That makes the post useful later, even when the original lab context has faded.

## Template

- What problem did you investigate?
- What spectrum, sweep, dataset, or artifact did you inspect?
- What changed as a result?
