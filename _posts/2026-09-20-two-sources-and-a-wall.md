Take two point sources that oscillate together. At every point in space their waves add up, and whether they reinforce or cancel depends only on how much further one wave travelled than the other.

## Path difference

For a screen far away, the path difference between the two sources at angle \( \theta \) is

\[
\Delta = d \sin\theta ,
\]

where \( d \) is the separation. Bright fringes appear when \( \Delta = m\lambda \) for integer \( m \), and dark ones when \( \Delta = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda \). Narrow the sources (smaller \( d \)) and the fringes spread out; shorten the wavelength and they pack together.

## A quick table

| Change | Fringe spacing |
| --- | --- |
| Larger separation \( d \) | Smaller |
| Longer wavelength \( \lambda \) | Larger |
| Both doubled | Unchanged |
